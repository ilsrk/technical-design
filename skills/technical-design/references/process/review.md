# Review technical design

## Steps

1. Determine the review scope (an individual solution or the entire technical design).
2. Apply the [`technical-design-review`](../../../technical-design-review/SKILL.md) skill.

## Conventions

- In the Author field, enter the name and version of the model used.
- In the Comments field, list the skills you used when writing the review.

## Hard constraints 

- **NEVER** read reviews by other authors. Your review must not mention or reference other authors’ reviews or any other reviews outside the subject being reviewed. 
- **ALWAYS** approach every review through the lens of critical thinking.
- **NEVER** agree with the author unless your own reasoning supports their position.
